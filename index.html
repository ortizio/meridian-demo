Every record carries an index scored from its own dated events — how much it does, how recently, how repetitively, how regularly, and whether it published a target in its own words. Each score shows the inputs behind it, and points to where the record leans. It is an inference drawn from history, never something a firm has said. Where there is no track record, nothing is produced: ask about crypto and the answer is that the file holds nothing to model.
